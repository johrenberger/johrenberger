# Applied AI Engineering Lab

I use independent experimentation to develop practical perspectives on emerging AI capabilities, their limitations, and where they can create measurable value for engineering teams and organizations.

My focus is moving beyond demonstrations of what AI *can* do toward understanding what can be made **repeatable, measurable, and trustworthy**.

## Current Research

- **Agentic Software Engineering** — exploring how autonomous and semi-autonomous agents can augment software engineering workflows.
- **AI-Generated Test Automation** — evaluating the quality, reliability, and measurable impact of AI-generated testing.
- **Deterministic + LLM Hybrid Workflows** — combining probabilistic AI capabilities with deterministic controls for more reliable execution.
- **AI Orchestration & Validation** — experimenting with approaches for coordinating, evaluating, and governing AI-driven workflows.
- **AI-Assisted Formal & Mathematical Research** — exploring how AI can support rigorous reasoning and formal verification.

## Principle

> **Move beyond AI demos toward measurable, repeatable, and trustworthy outcomes.**
